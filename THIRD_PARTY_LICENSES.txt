2. Stream-resolution engines
-------------------------------------------------------------------
InnerTubeX (com.github.MetrolistGroup.innertubex:innertubex-android:v0.11.7 —
bumped from v0.7.0 on 2026-11-14)
  GPL-3.0. https://github.com/MetrolistGroup/innertubex
  BitChord's stream-resolution engine (cipher tiers, client catalog,
  PoToken integration).

QuackJS (com.github.MetrolistGroup.quackjs:quackjs-android:0.1.4) —
transitive from InnerTubeX v0.11.x (runtime scope)
  GPL-3.0-only. https://github.com/MetrolistGroup/quackjs
  Kotlin-only YouTube player signature deciphering / n-transform engine.

NewPipe Extractor (com.github.TeamNewPipe:NewPipeExtractor:v0.26.3,
consumed as a re-jarred copy with org/schabi/newpipe/extractor/utils/Utils.class
stripped — see the stripNewPipeExtractorUtils task in app/build.gradle.kts)
  GPL-3.0-or-later. https://github.com/TeamNewPipe/NewPipeExtractor
  Failsafe stream resolver behind InnerTubeX.
